Very relaxed today, as if I’d put my worries out of mind for a while. Took some photos.
After a certain amount of pain, I fall into utter exhaustion, a state where I just want to lie flat.

But I really enjoy this state, because it never lasts long, and for the first time in a while I can find joy in ordinary pastimes. Before hardship finds me again, my anxiety eases.

It’s always hardship. Every stage has its own. I know this is an unhealthy way of thinking, but I can’t help myself.

Should I rest? Am I still not doing well enough? Rationally, I think it’s fine to stop and rest for a while.

But I can’t accept letting myself go. I’m like my own enemy, endlessly whipping and tormenting myself. I suppose I’d make a terrible parent, wouldn’t I? I can’t even tolerate my own decadence and incompetence, let alone my child’s. I don’t want to be that kind of parent.

If I ever have kids, it will be when I no longer have to worry about money.
