You’re disappointed in people, in society, in the world, but you still believe in purity, don’t you

Your perfectionism, your tightrope-walker’s sensitivity, your fear, your caution. You’re afraid. You can’t accept that something you could have done better was destroyed by your own hands, can you?

What you fear is a beautiful vision suddenly collapsing before your eyes, isn’t it?

You still don’t feel in control of anything you have, do you? You’re still afraid it could all vanish from your life in an instant, aren’t you?

What you fear is still the ordinariness of fading into the crowd, of losing the edge that makes you admired and loved, isn’t it?

You don’t want to be like most people, who have never thought about their own lives or what they want, do you?

You want freedom. You want a good quality of life. You want the whole world to be your warm bed, your comfort zone spread across this continent. You fear nothing, you fear everything, and no one can make you bow your head.

You crave tangible, material things. Your lover shouldn’t be the main theme, because the only one who can believe in you, who can accept you, will always be yourself.
