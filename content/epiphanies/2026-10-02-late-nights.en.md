I’ve been staying up late a lot lately.

Before college, my schedule was always regular. There was less to think about, and life ran on tracks. Even when I didn’t want to, well, I kept going. There was no choice.

In a sense, staying up late is my choice too. I like a life with choices.

There’s a slight pressure in my head. It’s become the norm, and it doesn’t surprise me, but reason tells me this isn’t right. If I keep this habit, I’ll slowly lose something.

It’s like a hidden debt: if I don’t keep my emotional spending in check, one day I won’t be able to pay it back.

Let me try to change that.
